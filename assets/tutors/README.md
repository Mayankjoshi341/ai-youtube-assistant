# Tutor Photos

Place your tutor/instructor photos in this folder.

## Supported formats
- `.png` (recommended — supports transparency)
- `.jpg` / `.jpeg`

## Naming convention
Use descriptive names so they are easy to identify in the UI:

```
tutor_01.png
tutor_02.png
sir_rahul.png
ma'am_priya.jpg
```

## How they are used
The thumbnail generator will:
1. Load the selected photo
2. Crop it into a circle
3. Place it on the right side of the 1280×720 YouTube thumbnail

## Tips
- Use a photo with a plain or simple background for best results
- Portrait orientation works best (face clearly visible)
- Minimum recommended size: 400×400 pixels
- The photo will be scaled to fit — no need to pre-crop

## Drop your photos here and they will automatically appear in the Tutor Selector in the app.
